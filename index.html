WBCHSE Rules
Class XI semester-system reference

Semester Structure

Class XI is conducted under the Semester I and Semester II system.

Semester I uses the prescribed MCQ-based examination pattern, while Semester II follows the conventional answer-script pattern.

Practical / Project components are completed as prescribed before the Semester II examination.

Promotion / Result

Students are required to satisfy the applicable Semester I and Semester II, theory and project/practical requirements.

The portal records the marks separately so that the school can maintain the examination record semester-wise.

Important

Always use the latest official WBCHSE notification, syllabus and examination instructions for final statutory decisions.
